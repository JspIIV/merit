GenLayer Asimov

A pass earned by a verified public action, not a self-claim.

An organiser opens a program with a requirement and one public page that establishes who qualifies. You claim your own pass, and the contract fetches that page and a round of GenLayer validators decides whether your address meets the requirement. Only then is the pass issued. Other contracts read has_pass to gate a fair airdrop, mint or vote on verified action, not a self-report or a farmable snapshot.
